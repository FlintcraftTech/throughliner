Can I keep my planning documents out of the repository, and does undo still work?

They already are, and undo works as usual. Your spec, your queue and your session records are tracked in the private outer repository of your nested project — the one that never gets a remote — so they are kept out of anything published while git still holds every version. Setup asks no keep-private question. The one exception is yours to ask for: a single document you want out of even local history can be gitignored, with this trade. What still works: Claude writes to it first and tells you what landed, because before each change the plugin saves a copy of the previous version into a local folder outside the repository, and a deleted queue item can be put back from there. What changes: /close cannot read its work on that document back from the file's history, so it records from what it remembers, and those saved copies live on this machine only — a lost disk loses them.
