Can a cycle run as a chain of steps with no dates?

Yes. You will meet this in planning when you ask to put a sequence of checklists on a cycle and say you want no dates on it. Claude writes the cycle's chain so that its first step fires on your word and each later step fires on the one before it; a step can also carry a condition, read from your session records, such as "one planning record and one build-run record dated after the chain's rezip record". Nothing in it is a date. From then on, your session opening's cycles line says which step is due and what it fires after — "[maintenance-sweep] is due, after [beta-pick]" — or that the chain is waiting on your word when its first step has no record yet, or that every step has its record and the next chain starts on your word. A step stays due until its own record exists; a step with a condition shows as held until the records say the condition holds. What tells you it worked: the opening names the chain as sequential and names a step, with no date beside it. A chain cannot be both: one with an anchor date and steps firing on one another is reported as malformed and nothing is filed from it.
