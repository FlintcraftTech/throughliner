What is a checklist, and how is it different from a cycle?

Both live in your cycles doc. A cycle is recurring work with a rhythm — a weekly release, a posting cadence — and the method works out when its next turn is due and puts that turn into your queue. A checklist is a step list with no rhythm: you save a procedure you repeat, and it runs when you ask for it in your own words — no word to remember — or when a cycle's chain reaches it. Your session opening names each checklist you have and what runs it. Ask in planning to save a repeated procedure as a checklist and it is written down with you there — or Claude offers it, once, where it has noticed you asking for the same sequence more than once; if it later turns out to have a rhythm, it becomes a cycle by gaining a cadence. A checklist whose steps write somewhere outside the usual project documents lists those paths in its definition, and they stay writable whenever the project is open, not only while the checklist runs — so the list is kept narrow.
