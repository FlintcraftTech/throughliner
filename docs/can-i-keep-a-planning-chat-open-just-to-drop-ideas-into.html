Can I keep a planning chat open just to drop ideas into?

Yes. You will meet this at the end of a planning session, when the queue has been worked through and Claude asks whether there is anything else to capture or discuss, then names the close command. Tell Claude, in whatever words you like, that you are keeping the chat open to capture ideas in while you work elsewhere. From then on, that question stops for the rest of the chat: each idea you type is discussed and filed, and the session does not keep asking you to wrap up. In an ordinary planning chat, where you have not said that, the question comes back each time new ideas fill the queue and it empties again. What tells you it worked: after your next idea is filed, Claude's reply ends without asking you to close. A safety check holds this: once Claude has understood you and written a small marker for the session, a reply that still ends by asking you to close is sent back for correction before you see it. As a fallback, a few fixed phrases ("keep the chat open", "keep capturing", "drop ideas into") arm the same check on their own, even where the marker was not written. Send the close command whenever you are done, and it records everything you dropped in.
