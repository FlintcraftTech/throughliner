Why did Claude start a helper without asking me first?

Because the helper runs on Haiku or Sonnet, the two smaller models the method lets through without a prompt. You meet this when Claude hands a bounded piece of reading to a helper in any session: a read of named files, a summary of one record, a check of a claim against the installed plugin, a draft of text you will see. The rules carry a fixed table of task kinds, each naming one of the two models, so Claude matches the task to a row and weighs nothing; a helper on any other model still asks you first, with its cost named, and a task on neither list is Claude's own. What tells you it worked: a Haiku helper's report carries facts and no recommendation, and a Sonnet helper's marks any recommendation as its own. Where a helper's report is unhelpful, the brief is the usual cause, and the rules say what a brief carries.
