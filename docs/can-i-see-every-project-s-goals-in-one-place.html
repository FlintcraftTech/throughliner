Can I see every project's goals in one place?

Yes, in Obsidian with the Tasks plugin, the same way you see every project's tasks. You meet this at setup, when the interview asks where the project is heading and how you would know it got there, and at any planning session that writes or rewrites a goal: each goal in your spec's Goals section is one checkbox line in the Tasks plugin's marks, - [ ] <the goal>, reached when <the test> (<project>) 🆔 <slug> #goal, with the #goal tag and never #user, so your task queries leave it out. A note of your own anywhere in the vault with a query of not done and tags include #goal lists every project's goals with their reached-when tests, and a skill of yours can read that note to weigh projects against each other; no session writes to that note. Tick the line when the goal is reached, or tell a planning session, and the goal is rewritten or removed from the spec as before, with its history kept in the session's record. A goal written in the older shape, a bold sentence with a "Reached when:" line beneath, gains its checkbox line at a planning opening on your yes. What tells you it worked: the query lists the goal, and after an edit to the spec a note names any goal line missing a part, the checkbox, the reached-when clause, the project, the 🆔 slug or the #goal tag, once while it stands.
