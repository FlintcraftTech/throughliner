Claude said a step I had approved was "blocked by classifier" — what do I do?

Switch the session to manual mode and ask for the step again. You meet this in Claude Code's auto mode, where a screen of Claude Code's own judges each action by its shape and refuses the ones it reads as destructive or outward — deleting a repository folder, transferring a repository, sending to another project, sometimes the ordinary write that follows one of those — whatever you said in chat. The refusal is Claude Code's, not the method's, and it tells Claude not to reach the same result another way, so the step stalls until you act. Switching to manual mode lets the approved step through, and a build run picks up where it stopped. Where the refused call was the tick that marks an item built, the item stays in the queue and the close names it; a fresh turn ticks it. What tells you it worked: the step runs with no refusal, and the run carries on.
