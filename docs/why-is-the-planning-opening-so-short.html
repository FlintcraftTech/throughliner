Why is the planning opening so short?

Because a long opening was not getting read. A planning session's first message is a finding sentence or two: how much work is cleared to run and waiting to be processed, held work as a count plus only the items whose hold changed, and one clause — "issues, replies, cycles and the rule checks: nothing" — for every check that found nothing, so you can see the checks ran without reading a list. The one question follows on its own line. After a build run, the opening leads with a few lines on what has changed for you since you last planned, each in your own words from when you asked for it, and the checks and counts come below a divider. The limit: a session that skipped a check could write the same clause.
