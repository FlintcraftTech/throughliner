Claude's reply was sent back for being too long — what happened?

You read a reply and, a moment later, a shorter one arrives saying the same thing. That is the stop check: it counts the prose in a finished reply — list lines, code blocks, quotes, headings and table rows left out — and where it runs past 175 words it sends the reply back once with the count and the fix, and Claude answers again shorter. It fires once per chat and then stays quiet, so a second long reply passes; the figure came from measuring the project's own chats, the 90th percentile of reply length, and is a constant in the hook rather than anything you set. Nothing to do on your side: read the shorter reply, which is the one that stands.
