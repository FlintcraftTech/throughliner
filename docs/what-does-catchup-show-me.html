What does /catchup show me?

A brief for coming back after time away. Type the catchup command in a chat that has opened, and you get one line per goal in your spec's Goals section — reached, or still the direction, with what the test shows now — then a few lines on what has changed for you in the last fourteen days: one line per change you will notice in use, each with a plain name, your own words from when you asked for it, what happens now and what it is for, six lines at most, with everything smaller gathered into one closing line. Then three lines: what a build run would do next, what is waiting on you, and what is held on a date. It also runs the checks a fresh chat runs at its opening, which matters in a chat you picked up days later: one line each where a cycle is due now, the plugin changed version under the chat, or the date has moved, and nothing where none of those holds — it files a due cycle's capture exactly as an opening would, and moves nothing in your queue. It ends on one offer to walk you through any of the changes, one message each. Setup asks for the goals — where the project is heading and how you would know it got there — and an existing project is asked once by the top-up.
