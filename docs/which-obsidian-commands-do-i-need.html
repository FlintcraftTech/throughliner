Which Obsidian commands do I need?

Only a few. Open Obsidian's command palette with Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on a Mac), type the first letters of a command's name, and press Enter. These are the ones Throughliner sends you to, named as the palette shows them:

  • Quick switcher: Open quick switcher — opens a file by typing its name: a draft Claude hands you to edit, a project's QUEUE.md, your task-list note.
  • Toggle reading view — switches a note between its text and its finished view. Use it on your task-list note to see each query as a live list of tasks.
  • Tasks: Toggle task done — ticks the task line your cursor is on and stamps the date, which is how a step of yours is marked done. Needs the Tasks plugin; clicking the checkbox does the same.

Everything else Throughliner asks of you is typed in Claude Code, not in Obsidian.
