Can I see every project's queue in one place?

Yes, in Obsidian with the Tasks plugin, where your projects sit inside one vault. Every entry in every queue carries one checkbox line directly under its heading, written and kept current by the tools and never by hand: the entry's description, the project's name in brackets, 🆔 with its slug, ⛔ with the slugs of what it waits on, and 📅 with its date where it has one. A Tasks query such as not done over the vault lists every entry in every project with what it waits on, so a dependency between two projects shows in one place. The line is derived from the entry's own fields, so ticking it changes nothing for Claude's work; a step of yours is the exception, whose line is its own task, carries the #user tag, and whose tick the next planning session reads. A query of not done with tags include #user shows those steps alone.
